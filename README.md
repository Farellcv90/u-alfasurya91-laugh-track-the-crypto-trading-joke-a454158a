One line, under 200 characters, about crypto and trading life — written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Bought every dip until the dip bought me — now I'm the floor, and the group chat walks on me for support.

## What the joke does

The setup is every trader's favourite mantra — "buy the dip" — and the turn flips buyer and bought: the dip ends up owning *him*. The punchline lands in the last words, with "support" doing double duty: the group chat literally walks on him, and in chart terms he has *become* the support level. A recognisable trading-life detail (the group chat, the floor, support) keeps it earned rather than generic, and the line is short enough to repeat to a friend.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It validates the line (single line, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
